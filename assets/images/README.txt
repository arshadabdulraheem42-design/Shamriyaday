PHOTOS & IMAGES DIRECTORY (assets/images/)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Welcome! This folder holds the photos displayed in the "Our Story"
polaroids, the "Gallery Wall", the "Memory Lane Recap", and social
share preview (og-cover.jpg).

HOW TO USE YOUR OWN PHOTOS:
1. Copy your photos into this folder (e.g. photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, etc.).
2. Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
3. Open `js/config.js` in VS Code and update the image paths:
   e.g. `image: "assets/images/photo1.jpg"`

TIPS FOR BEST LOOK & SPEED:
- Recommended dimensions: 1200px wide for landscape, 1000px wide for portrait.
- File size: Keep under 300KB-500KB per photo for instant loading on mobile.
- Free compression tool: https://squoosh.app or https://tinypng.com
- Fallback: If any photo file is missing or mistyped, the site will
  automatically show an elegant gradient heart placeholder instead of a
  broken icon!
